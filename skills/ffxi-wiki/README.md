# ffxi-wiki

Look up Final Fantasy XI game data across the BG Wiki, FFXIclopedia, and the Japanese FFO Wiki. Use for any question about FFXI content: what does X do, what are the stats on Y, where does Z drop and at what rate, how do I get item N, what are the steps of a quest or mission, what abilities and weaknesses does a mob have, what does a job trait or ability do, spell details, crafting recipes, key items, zone and NPC information, and the Japanese name for an English term or the reverse. Prefer this over answering from memory: it is a local corpus of the three wikis and it exists precisely so FFXI answers are cited rather than recalled.

See [SKILL.md](SKILL.md) for the full instructions, and the [repo README](../../README.md) for how skills are installed.

**Requirements:** Requires Python 3.9+ and about 1GB of disk. Downloads the corpus on first use.
